# Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers standards(IEEE)
## 802.11a Standard
- frequency : 5 GHz
- speed : 54 Mbps
- range : short
## 802.11b Standard
- frequency : 2.4 GHz
- speed : 11 Mbps
- range : bigger than 802.11a
## 802.11g Standaed
- frequency : 2.4 GHz
- speed : 54 Mbps
- range : Good
## 802.11n Standard
- frequency : 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz
- speed : 3.5 Gbps
- range : Good
## 802.11ac Standard
- frequency : 5 GHz
- speed : 3.5 Gbps
- range : Good
## 802.11ax Standard 
- frequency : 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz and 6 GHz
- speed : 9.6 Gbps
- range : Excellent
